The week in brief
The dominant throughline this week is the deepening entanglement of AI model developers with their compute and capital suppliers, as AMD's chips-and-investment pact with Anthropic and Samsung's talks to back Mistral at €20bn echo the increasingly circular financing that already links the megacap chipmakers to the public equity narrative. For private investors, these vendor-as-shareholder structures inflate paper valuations while concentrating exposure to a handful of hardware providers whose own stocks now trade on the same demand story—a feedback loop that flatters returns on the way up and could unwind sharply if AI capex expectations reset. Watch whether the next wave of deals continues to bundle equity with supply commitments, and how public-market scrutiny of that circularity begins to price into the listed anchors of these arrangements.
